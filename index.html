Privacy & how we use your data

Last updated: October 2026 · CanteenConnect, Silver Oak University

What we collect

  • Your name — shown with your reviews
  • Your Google account e-mail address — used only to identify your account
  • Your reviews — dish rating, text, and any photo you choose to add
  • Basic usage data — which dishes are opened, so we can show what is popular

What we do NOT do

  • We never sell your data to anyone
  • We never show your e-mail address to other students or to the canteen
  • We never post on your behalf
  • Canteen staff cannot post, edit or delete reviews — they can only respond

Photos you upload

If you add a photo of your food, please make sure no person's face is clearly visible without their permission. Do not upload ID cards, receipts with personal details, or anything you would not want made public.

Who can see what

  • Students — all reviews, ratings and dish photos
  • Canteen manager — ratings and feedback summary, plus reported reviews
  • College administration — the same management dashboard, for hygiene and service quality

Your choices

  • You can delete any of your own reviews at any time from the "My Reviews" tab
  • You can change the display name on your reviews
  • To delete your account and everything in it, ask the canteen manager or write to us

Keeping it honest

Reviews are tied to one real account per person. Anyone posting abusive, spam or deliberately false content may have their reviews hidden after being reported by other students.

Contact

Questions? Speak to the canteen manager, or contact the CanteenConnect student team at Silver Oak University.

This page is written in plain language on purpose — you should be able to understand exactly what happens to your data without being a lawyer.
